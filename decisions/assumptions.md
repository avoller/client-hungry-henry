## ASM-001 — Fitness Enthusiast count equals US fitness-facility membership

- **Claim:** 81 million US Fitness Enthusiasts, using 2025 HFA facility membership as the proxy.
- **Status:** open
- **Owner:** Alex Voller
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Basis:** HFA 2025 membership is the closest published count of people in a structured fitness practice. It excludes enthusiasts who never join a facility and includes members who do not watch nutrition.
- **Test:** Survey 200 gym members and 200 self-identified fitness enthusiasts on whether they keep a nutrition plan. Adjust the 81 million figure if the overlap is under 70% or over 95%.
- **Depends on it:** Workbook §1.8 total impact (3.2 billion meals); §2.1 market size; all segment sizes.
- **Raised:** 2026-09-21
- **Resolved:** —
