# Hungry Henry voice

## How we name people

Say **Fitness Enthusiast** for the person using the consumer product. In interface copy, address them as **you**.

Do not substitute *user*, *diner*, *customer*, or *consumer* for that person. Restaurant-side copy may say **restaurant** or **operator**.

## How the name is written

**Hungry Henry.** Two words, both capitalised. It does not take *the*. Never *HungryHenry*, *HH*, or *the Hungry Henry*.

## Tone

Three adjectives. One sentence each.

**Direct.** Tell them the meal is a match, or it is not — do not soften a miss into a maybe.

**Appetising.** Write as if Friday night is allowed: mains, sides, and a plate you actually want, not a ration.

**Unashamed.** A cheat meal is the point. Do not apologise for eating out, and do not lecture.

## Words we do not use

- *guilty pleasure* (the product is guilt-free; guilt is the problem, not the flavour)
- *diet-friendly* or *sin-free*
- *swipe right* / *Tinder for food* (the motion is bump-in / bump-out of food items, not a card stack)
- *user* as a noun in client-facing copy
